## Rules

1. Follow Discord's [Terms of Service](<https://discord.com/terms>) and [Community Guidelines](<https://discord.com/guidelines>). You must be 13 or older, or the minimum age in your country.
2. Don't share or sell cheats or hacks.
3. No spam: repeated messages, mass mentions, or the same post in several channels.
4. Ask for help in the server, and open a ticket for anything private. Don't DM or ping staff for support.
5. Staff decisions stand. To appeal one, open a ticket.

Breaking a rule earns a warning, timeout, kick or ban, depending on what it was.
