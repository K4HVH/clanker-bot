## Medius

Replacement firmware for the MAKCU box. It clones your mouse or keyboard to the PC byte for byte, and gives software a faster control protocol.

**Get going**
- Install from the browser: https://medius.k4tech.net/dashboard/setup
- Update: <https://medius.k4tech.net/dashboard/update>
- Does your mouse or keyboard work? <https://medius.k4tech.net/guide/compatibility>
- Options: <https://medius.k4tech.net/guide/options>
- Help: <https://medius.k4tech.net/guide/help>
- For developers: <https://medius.k4tech.net/native>
